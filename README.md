# My favorite movie as of 2023
## Super Mario Movie
> **Distributed by:** Universal Pictures
> **Producted by:** Nintendo, Universal Pictures, Illumination
> **Based on:** Super Mario games and classic cartoons
> **Released on:** April 1, 2023
> **Budget:** $100,000,000
> **Characters:** Mario, Luigi, Princess Peach, Toad, Donkey Kong, Bowser
> **Plot (in my own words):** Mario and Luigi started a job as plumbers but ended up failing. As they see an opportunity like a flooded city, they will find out what's happening. As they go in the sewers, the brothers find the portal to another world instead.
